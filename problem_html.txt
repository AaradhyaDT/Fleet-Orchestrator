<p>In the magical kingdom of Numberia, a young wizard named Alex has discovered an ancient calculator that can perform only one spell: <strong>adding two big numbers</strong>.</p>

<p>One day, the kingdom's royal mathematician gives Alex several number puzzles to solve. For every puzzle, Alex receives two integers, and the calculator reveals their sum.</p>

<p>However, the wizard is still learning programming and needs your help!</p>

<p>Your task is to write a program that reads the number of test cases <strong>T</strong>. For each test case, you are given two integers <strong>A</strong> and <strong>B</strong>. Calculate and print their sum.</p>

<p>Help Alex complete all the puzzles and become the greatest number wizard in Numberia!</p>

<h3>Example</h3>

<p>If the given numbers are 5 and 7, their sum is 12.</p>
